A workspace is the world zone that contains the following:

Manufacturing Facility+subareas
Office+subareas

Think of it like a campus that the player can move about freely in to access all the manufacturing facility subareas and office subareas.

The initial workspace is your Home

Home Workspace:
-Manufacturing Facility
--shop floor: garage
--storage area: backyard shed
--shipping & arrival areas: driveway

-Office: Home Office
--Computer

Further workspaces are upgrades to be rented or bought.
Upgrading a workspace provides a larger manufacturing facility, with upgraded shipping & arrival areas.
